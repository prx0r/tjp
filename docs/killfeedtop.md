# KILLFEED TOP — five binaries per trade (2026-09-12, saved word for word)

> Every scarcity trade has exactly five binary statements that must remain TRUE.
> The instant a high-ranked statement flips FALSE, it enters the kill feed.
> Not probabilities first. Not 40 indicators. Five conditions that make the trade logically possible.

## Universal five (ranked)

1. **Need** — downstream still needs X? (nuclear button; uranium refinement: activity exists AND requires X)
2. **Gap** — demand > *effective* supply? (fiber: downstream boomed while capacity boomed more)
3. **Lag** — supply still slow to respond? (market may peak before physical scarcity does)
4. **WTP** — buyers still price-insensitive? Observable: price ↑ quantity unchanged = bullish;
   price ↑ quantity ↓ = kill feed (rare earths).
5. **Substitution absence** — no cheaper workaround? (palladium murdered here; fiber hurt by DWDM; oil by conservation)

## States + evidence discipline

Each condition: TRUE / WATCH / FALSE + evidence. TRUE→WATCH on first serious counter-evidence
(Micron +60% early, Samsung qualified, Hynix fab early); WATCH→FALSE on confirmation
(TrendForce supply > requirements) → KillFeed fires.

## Five magic questions (discovery)

Why must anyone buy this? Why can't they get enough? Why can't someone quickly make more?
Why don't buyers care about price? Why can't they use something else? All compelling = candidate.
Then monitor the same five in reverse — first serious change begins the exit.
Deterministic causal monitoring, not an investment-news feed.
