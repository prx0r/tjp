# agichat — Post-AGI trade thesis (2026-09-12 chat, saved word for word)

## The core Post-AGI trade

The thesis is not simply:

> AI gets better → buy AI companies.

It is:

> **Cognition gets cheaper → the economic value migrates toward whatever cognition cannot cheaply manufacture.**

As reasoning approaches commodity pricing, the scarce complements become:

**secrecy → privacy / encrypted state**
**trust → verification / provenance / identity**
**permission → certification / regulation / authorization**
**reality → measurements / sensors / experiments**
**failure information → rare negative data**
**physical throughput → fabs / cleanrooms / power / water / manufacturing slots**
**cryptographic durability → post-quantum security**

Evidence over philosophy: EU Digital Product Passport registry live **July 20, 2026**, mandatory
battery passports from **February 18, 2027** ([IMIES][1]). Transcat quarter: calibration-service
revenue **+27%** on regulated-industry demand. Confidential-computing groups framing protected
execution as agent infrastructure ([CCC][2]).

## GOLD trade, not just good company (NIL lesson)

Incredible company ≠ trade. NIL works because: ~$20m-ish cap asymmetry, legible narrative
(privacy + agents + encrypted compute), near-term catalyst (mainnet/network), reflexivity already
moving, accessible venue (Hyperliquid), psychologically natural $0.10 target, non-insane terminal valuation.


85–100 = GOLD (trade). 75–84 = READY (monitor). 60–74 = WATCH (setup wrong). <60 = research.

- Scarcity migration: does stronger AI *increase* this asset's shadow value? (NIL privacy, verification, calibration, power.)
- Valuation convexity: $20m→$50m is easy; $20B→$50B needs extraordinary capital.
- Catalyst proximity: mainnet, upgrade, regulation date, listing, launch, sellout, audit, earnings. No catalyst = research idea.
- Reflexivity: 7d/30d RS, higher lows, volume accel, vol/mcap, funding, OI, social, listings, holds-not-roundtrips.
  DUSK ~$0.0645 (Aug 30) → ~$0.080 (Sep 6), ~$8m daily vs ~$47m mcap = real turnover ([CoinGecko][3]).
  ALEO ~$0.017 / ~$24m = dead ([CoinGecko][4]). Same cheapness, different trade.
- Liquidity/access: QRL thesis excellent (~$55m, audit 55% Sep 11, [QRL][5]) but Sep 10 ~$36k
  and Sep 11 ~$12k turnover ([CoinGecko][6]) blocks GOLD.
- Narrative compression: one sentence. "Nillion lets agents compute without revealing secrets." Good.
  Japanese-subsystem-supply-chain sentences don't reprice reflexively.
- Falsifiability: state what observation admits wrongness, or ThesisDesk becomes story-preservation.

## Scarce complements have derivatives

Stronger question than "what is scarce": **what becomes scarcer as AI gets better?**

$$
S_i = \frac{\partial \text{economic value of complement}_i}{\partial \text{AI capability}}
$$

Positive: private state, authentication/provenance, physical identity (DPP IDs/APIs), calibration
(+27% Transcat), reliable power *now* (time-to-power, [TechRadar][7]). Negative: coding, generic content, synthesis.

> **Don't predict which AI becomes godlike. Predict which scarce inputs every sufficiently
